| E-03 | 2026-11-26 | narration | The challenge's rule of thumb (2–2.5 spoken words/s, so 130–220 words for 60–90 s) will hold for Amharic TTS. | Generated all 9 narration lines (63 Amharic words) with `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, voice Kore, style "warm, calm, unhurried, like an elder telling a family story"; measured each WAV. | **Rejected.** 63 words took **55.73 s**, so **≈1.13 words/s**, about half the English rule of thumb. | E-04 | 2026-11-26 | radio cut (assembly) | A single-pass `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=11` on the final mix is accurate enough to hit −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP. | Built `outputs/radio_cut/radio_cut.mp4` with one-pass loudnorm after voice + ducked drone mix, then measured with `ebur128=peak=true`. | **Missed:** integrated **−15.4 LUFS** (1.4 LU quiet) and true peak **−0.9 dBFS** (0.1 dB over the ceiling). | E-05 | 2026-11-26 | reference sheet | (Re-test of E-01, fair this time.) | E-06 | 2026-11-26 | keyframes | A fixed **object glossary** entry for "jebena" (round bulbous base, long narrow neck, small spout, handle, woven ring stand), appended whenever a prompt mentions a jebena, will fix the teapot/jug problem seen in all 4 reference images (E-05). | Added `style.object_glossary.jebena` to the storyboard (v4); `cloudflare.build_prompt` appends it automatically; generated all 10 keyframes with FLUX.1 schnell. | **Confirmed.** The 3 shots that mention a jebena (s05, s06, s10) all show a dark round clay pot with a long neck; s10 even has the woven ring stand. 0/4 reference images (without the glossary) had a recognisable jebena. | `outputs/keyframes/s05,s06,s10.png`; `runs.jsonl` stage `keyframes` | Keep the glossary.
